Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information may be processed when you use this game, why it is used, and how to contact FatCat Game Studio.

Last updated: 2026-11-08

Information you provide and data on your device

The game does not ask you to create an account or enter your name, email address, contacts, or precise location through an in-game profile. Game progress and settings may be stored on your device or through platform features, depending on the game and version. FatCat Game Studio does not operate an account profile for these games.

Technical, gameplay, and diagnostic data

When the services listed below are enabled in the installed version, their SDKs may process technical/device identifiers (such as an app installation or advertising identifier, device and operating-system information, IP address), app usage and gameplay events, and crash or performance diagnostics. These services process data to provide analytics, diagnostics, advertising, or purchase support. We do not use these events to ask for or collect player-written content.

Advertising

If this game shows ads, the ad mediation and its configured advertising partners may process device and ad interaction data to deliver, measure, prevent fraud on, and, where allowed by platform settings and applicable law, personalize ads. The exact partners can vary by platform, region, consent choice, and release build. Use your device privacy/ad settings and the in-game consent controls where available.

Purchases

If the game offers in-app purchases, Apple or Google processes payment details. FatCat Game Studio does not receive your full payment card details. RevenueCat may process a pseudonymous app-user identifier, store receipt or purchase token, product and entitlement status, and technical information to validate purchases and restore entitlements. Purchase availability depends on the game.

Children and family audiences

Some FatCat games are designed for children or families. The game does not ask a child to submit direct contact details. Depending on the game and build, technical, gameplay, ad, or purchase services described above may still process device or usage data. Parents or guardians can contact us with questions or requests. The store age rating and family-program listing apply to the specific store version.

Who receives data

FatCat Game Studio does not sell personal information. Data is sent only to the service providers enabled in the particular game build and to the platform that distributes the game, for the purposes described here. Providers may process data under their own privacy terms and may act as service providers or independent controllers.

  • Unity LevelPlay (ironSource)
  • RevenueCat
  • Google Play services

Retention, choices, and deletion

FatCat Game Studio does not operate an account system for these games. Progress stored by the game can generally be removed by clearing its data or uninstalling it, although platform or cloud backups may behave differently. Analytics, ad, crash, and purchase records are retained and deleted according to the relevant provider’s controls and policies; those periods vary by provider and service. You can change device advertising/privacy settings and any in-game consent choices offered. To ask about a data request, email us; we will direct it to the relevant provider where we can identify the data and have a means to do so.

Security and external links

Network connections to service providers use the transport and security controls provided by the game SDKs and platform. No internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Links to external services are governed by those services’ own policies.

Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy when the game, its providers, or legal requirements change. The updated version and date will be posted on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact FatCat Game Studio at fatcatgamestudio.oc@gmail.com.
