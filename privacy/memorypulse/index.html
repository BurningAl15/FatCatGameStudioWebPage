Memory Pulse

Privacy Policy

This policy applies to Memory Pulse for Android (package com.FatCatGameStudio.MemoryPulse). It explains what the game stores locally and what may be processed to complete the one-time Remove Ads purchase.

Last updated: 2026-11-11

Game data and local storage

Memory Pulse has Classic, Time Attack, and Survival modes. Game progress and preferences are stored locally on the device. The game does not provide player accounts, chat, or user-created content, and does not ask for a name, email address, contacts, or precise location.

Remove Ads purchase

The one-time Remove Ads purchase is handled through Google Play and RevenueCat Unity SDK 9.10.1. The game configures RevenueCat without a custom account ID, so RevenueCat creates an anonymous App User ID. To verify and restore the purchase, RevenueCat may process that identifier, the Google Play purchase token and related transaction/product and entitlement status, and standard app/platform information such as device type, operating-system version, app version, locale, and currency. A request IP address may be used to serve the request and infer country; RevenueCat says it drops the IP after determining country. No email, name, or phone number is provided by the game to RevenueCat.

Advertising and notifications in the reviewed Android build

The latest local Android release artifact reviewed is version 1.0.2, build 2. LevelPlay and Unity Ads libraries are present, but the Android ad configuration still contains template values and the game code stops ad initialization when the app key is a placeholder. Ads therefore are not initialized by this reviewed build, and no ad request is expected from it. Unity Push Notifications and Firebase Messaging dependencies are also present, but the reviewed project has no Firebase app configuration and no push-registration or Firebase Messaging initialization call. Push tokens and notification interaction events are therefore not expected to be sent by this build. If a store-distributed build has different configuration, this policy and the store disclosures must be updated before that build is released.

Retention and deletion

Local progress and preferences remain on the device until you clear the app data or uninstall the game; device or platform backups may retain their own copies. RevenueCat keeps purchase and entitlement records under its own retention and deletion policies. You can ask us to locate and request deletion of an identifiable RevenueCat record by emailing the contact below. Google Play transaction records are handled under Google’s policies and controls. We do not state a fixed retention period because the service providers determine it under their policies and applicable requirements.

Security

The game uses the security controls provided by Android, Google Play, and its SDK providers. RevenueCat states that data it collects is encrypted in transit. Local game data is stored on your device and is subject to your device’s security and backup settings. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Questions and privacy requests

For questions, access or deletion requests, or concerns about this policy, contact FatCat Game Studio at the email below. Please do not include payment-card details in your message. We may need enough information to identify the relevant purchase record before we can route a request to a provider.

Third parties that process purchase data

Google Play processes the store transaction. RevenueCat receives purchase information to verify and restore the Remove Ads entitlement. The game does not receive full payment-card details.
