Fox’s Tale

Privacy Policy

This policy applies to Fox’s Tale (Android package and Apple bundle identifier: com.fatcatgamestudio.foxstale), a 2D pixel-art platform game. It describes the data handled by the game and the third-party services integrated in the reviewed Android and iOS release artifacts.

Last updated: 2026-11-11

Game information stored on your device

Fox’s Tale does not require you to sign in or create an account. The game stores progress, level state, collectibles, lives, and settings locally on your device. The reviewed game code does not send this local save data to a FatCat Game Studio account service. The game does not provide chat or user-created content.

Advertising and ad measurement

The reviewed release artifacts include Unity LevelPlay Unity package 9.5.1 and Unity Ads components. The Android dependency declarations specify mediation SDK 9.7.0 and Unity Ads adapter 5.15.0; the iOS dependency declarations specify IronSource SDK 9.7.0 and Unity Ads adapter 5.13.0. The game contains configured app and ad-unit identifiers for both platforms. Whether an ad is available or displayed can depend on connectivity, region, store build, ad inventory, purchase status, and provider settings. When an ad request or impression is handled, LevelPlay, Unity Ads, and the advertising partners selected for that request may process an advertising or device identifier (such as Android Advertising ID or Apple’s advertising identifier where available), IP address, approximate location inferred from IP, device and operating-system details, app version, ad delivery and interaction events, and information used for security, fraud prevention, and service diagnostics. The partner and exact data fields can vary by request and configuration.

Remove Ads purchase

Fox’s Tale offers a one-time Remove Ads purchase (Google Play product ID: com.fatcatgamestudio.foxstale.noads). RevenueCat Unity SDK 9.15.0 is integrated and is used to check the purchase and restore the entitlement. The game does not configure a custom RevenueCat user ID, so the SDK uses an anonymous App User ID. RevenueCat and the store may process purchase or restore information such as this identifier, a store transaction receipt or purchase token, product and entitlement status, and app or device technical information. Google Play or Apple processes the payment. FatCat Game Studio does not receive your full payment-card details through this integration.

Analytics, notifications, and other services

The reviewed project source contains a local AnalyticsManager and a conditionally compiled Unity Analytics call, but Unity Services Analytics is not listed as a project package and no Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics package or app configuration was found. The current artifacts therefore do not provide sufficient evidence that gameplay analytics or Firebase Analytics/Crashlytics is active, so this policy does not describe those as active services. Firebase Messaging and Unity Push Notifications were also not found as integrated packages or initialized services in the reviewed release artifacts. If a distributed build differs, this policy must be updated to match it.

Information we do not ask you to provide

The game does not require your name, email address, contacts, account credentials, or precise location to play. Do not include payment-card details in privacy requests. The stores and service providers may process information under their own policies when you use their services.

How information is used and shared

Local game data supports gameplay and saves progress on your device. LevelPlay and its configured ad network(s) use ad-related data to request, deliver, measure, and protect advertising services. RevenueCat and the applicable store use purchase information to validate and restore the Remove Ads entitlement. Data is shared with those providers only as needed for the corresponding service; their own privacy notices explain their processing. FatCat Game Studio does not operate a player account backend for this game.

Retention and deletion

Local progress and settings remain on your device until you clear the game’s storage or uninstall it; device or platform backups may retain separate copies. RevenueCat, the advertising providers, and the stores retain service records under their own policies and controls. We do not set or promise a fixed retention period for records held by those providers. You may contact us at the address below to ask about a privacy request; include enough information for us to identify the relevant service or purchase, but do not send payment-card details. We will direct a request to a provider when we can identify the relevant record and have a means to do so. Store transaction records are managed by the relevant store.

Security

Game progress is stored on your device and is subject to your device’s security and backup settings. Network processing for purchases and advertising is handled by the relevant platform and SDK provider using their security measures. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Privacy contact

For questions or privacy requests, contact FatCat Game Studio using the email address published on the official studio website. Please do not include payment-card details.
